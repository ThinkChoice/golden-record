Sensism: A Work in Progress.

Sensism is a personal philosophy project about orientation under incomplete knowledge. This is a current working presentation, not a declaration of final doctrine. The Bones are the compressed core; the Flesh is still being tested and compressed against philosophical questions.

The bones

Perspective matters.
Confront. Reason. Respond.

Objectivity voids.
It is nonconfrontational, unreasonable, irresponsible.

Probability condemns Absurdity.
Absurdity smiles.
Objectivity voids Possibility.

Perspective senses Logos.
Logos means Question.
Objectivity voids Meaning.

The flesh

The current compression pass asks a philosophical question and then looks for the smallest Sensist response the Bones can actually support. Different questions may receive the same response; the point is consistency, not manufactured novelty.

Ethics

Question: Does mattering depend upon winning, survival, rescue, sympathy, or favorable outcome?

Sensism responds: Perspective matters.

Ethics

Question: Does sympathy or suffering grant a Perspective sovereignty over competing Perspectives?

Sensism responds: Perspective matters.

Political / Social Philosophy

Question: How does external constraint affect Perspective?

Sensism responds: Confront.

Existential Philosophy

Question: What remains available to Perspective under extreme constraint?

Sensism responds: Respond.

Metaphysics

Question: When, if ever, does constraint become impossibility?

Sensism responds: Objectivity voids Possibility.

Epistemology

Question: What can Perspective know about its own experience?

Sensism responds: Perspective matters. Sensism is a Perspective.

Still a work in progress

The Flesh is being revisited one branch question at a time. The current next question concerns philosophy of language: how Sensism approaches meaning when language admits multiple interpretations. Other established ideas remain available for later compression, but this public page follows the current lean codification rather than publishing the much larger developmental genealogy.

Sensism remains revisable. Its private working history, committee discussions, discarded formulations and unresolved questions are intentionally not reproduced here.
