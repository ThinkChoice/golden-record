Sensism: A Work in Progress.

Sensism is a personal philosophy project about orientation under incomplete knowledge. This is a current working presentation, not a declaration of final doctrine.

The bones

Perspective matters.
Confront. Reason. Respond.

Objectivity voids.
It is nonconfrontational, unreasonable, irresponsible.

Probability condemns Absurdity.
Absurdity smiles.
Objectivity voids Possibility.

Perspective senses Logos.
Logos means Question.
Objectivity voids Meaning.

Selected developing flesh

Logos means Question

A perspective can sense something as meaningful, patterned or significant without that encounter proving the interpretation correct. The sensed Logos opens a question; it does not close one.

Weighing without an ultimate scale

Reason can compare evidence, coherence, probability, consequences, relevance, confidence and competing claims without pretending to possess a perspective-free final hierarchy.

Mattering without guaranteed victory

Perspective matters does not promise that a perspective wins, survives, is rescued or turns out to be true. Losing does not revoke mattering.

Sympathy without sovereignty

Sympathy matters as Perspective, but it does not receive automatic authority. It enters confrontation and reasoning alongside other perspectives.

Constraint without Objectivity

Institutions, bodies, coercion, deprivation and material conditions can radically narrow what a perspective can do without Sensism claiming that Possibility itself has been objectively exhausted.

Aresponse

Response need not look like conventional action. Silence, abstention, refusal, incapacity, inanimation or death do not thereby become Objectivity.

Relation

Perspectives relate. Relationships are Perspectives. Relationships matter. Perspectives are relatable.

Still unfinished

The Flesh is actively being compressed and tested. The current roadmap still includes the treatment of mistakes as Perspectives and the relationship between probability, Response and Objectivity, followed by a broader compression pass. The Bones remain frozen during that work.

The private developmental genealogy is much larger. This public page intentionally carries only a very small amount of it.
