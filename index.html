SENSISM

CURRENT CANON

Perspective matters.

Confront. Reason. Respond.

CENTRAL PROBLEM

Human beings must orient themselves within a reality they cannot fully know and among questions they cannot finally settle.

Sensism does not answer this by pretending uncertainty disappears. Neither does uncertainty eliminate the need to live.

We may not know the destination. We still have to find our bearings.

PERSPECTIVE MATTERS

Perspective belongs inside the work of confronting, reasoning, and responding.

“Matters” does not mean every perspective is true, equally credible, supreme, immune from challenge, or capable of defeating evidence.

Perspective has standing. Confront and Reason still distinguish evidence, credibility, relevance, confidence, consequences, assumptions, competing claims, institutions, incentives, relationships, history, material conditions, emotion, vulnerability, and power.

CONFRONT

Encounter what is actually present without manufacturing certainty.

Question. Notice. Examine. Listen. Update.

Distinguish knowledge from inference, belief, hope, fear, ideology, convention, and assumption. Question the framing as well as proposed answers. Examine the map as well as the terrain.

REASON

Incomplete knowledge does not license arbitrariness.

Consider. Distinguish. Compare. Calibrate. Reconsider.

Examine evidence, explanations, assumptions, contradictions, values, alternatives, and consequences. Distinguish confidence from certainty. Calibrate the strength and reversibility of a response to justification, stakes, urgency, and costs of error.

RESPOND

Life does not wait for complete metaphysical certainty.

Choose. Commit. Care. Bear. Repair. Reorient.

Action and inaction both have consequences. Respond while remaining answerable to those consequences.

FEEDBACK

Confront → Reason → Respond → confront the consequences → reason again → reorient.

Sensism itself receives no exemption from this method. Criticism, failure, contradiction, and consequence may require Sensism itself to change.

OBJECTIVITY / VOID

Perspective matters. It confronts, reasons, and responds.

Objectivity voids. It is nonconfrontational, unreasonable, and irresponsible.

“Objectivity” here refers to a hypothetical perspective-less condition, not ordinary impartiality or careful attempts to reduce bias. The formulation does not simply declare objectivity false. Perspectives may reason about objectivity without claiming possession of a perspective-free standpoint.

ABSURDITY / POSSIBILITY

Probability condemns Absurdity.
Absurdity smiles.
Objectivity voids Possibility.

A perspective may judge something overwhelmingly improbable without converting probability into certainty. The improbable remainder retains standing as possibility.

THE ROBBY TABLETS

The Robby Tablets are durable, living project artifacts developed so that work can survive individual ChatGPT conversations.

Conversation is workshop space.
The Tablet is durable working structure.

Preserve state, not transcript.

The Robby Tablet Constitution governs the system. ROBBY is the canonical spelling. A future authorized conversation encountering the Robby Tablet system should locate the Constitution, identify the relevant authoritative Tablet, read its orientation/README and current checkpoint, and continue from preserved state rather than forcing Robby to reconstruct old conversations.

AUTO-SAVE

Auto-save is a behavior, not a destination.

Material advances should be distilled into their natural authoritative Tablet or module. A checkpoint should preserve the current usable edge: durable conclusions, active working conclusions, tentative hypotheses, unresolved questions, corrections, and next steps.

PUBLIC VOYAGE

Public Voyage is the deliberate process by which selected material crosses from private developmental work into public exposure.

Publication does not mean finality.

Maturity, privacy, publication, and completion are separate dimensions.

Outside reaction becomes perspective or evidence to confront and reason through. It is not automatic authority.

GOLDEN RECORD

Golden Record is the Robby Tablet/project responsible for solving this outward communication problem.

The name follows the Voyager analogy: finite bandwidth makes selection important. The goal is not to transmit every conversation. The goal is to carry enough identity, philosophy, architecture, provenance, and routing information that the transmission remains intelligible after separation from its creators.

The project developed through an exploratory “Robby Voyage” phase analogous to the Grand Tour concept that preceded Voyager. Experiments have included Dropbox durable storage, Google Sites, and Google Apps Script. Those experiments clarified that storage and human publication were achievable, while direct machine retrieval remained the missing communications link.

This static page is the next experiment.

SECURITY POSTURE

The public surface should be intentionally boring where practical.

Prefer static, read-only publication with no public login, database, comments, forms, uploads, or private credentials merely for the sake of publication.

The public representation should not need a pathway back into private Google Drive, Gmail, Dropbox, financial data, or other private systems.

These are defaults and guardrails, not irreversible commandments. Robby retains human authority to deliberately change the architecture after considering tradeoffs.
