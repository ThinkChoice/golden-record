Week 5 football notebook.

Developing observations about the games and players that catch my attention this week. Notes will grow as the week unfolds.

Opening notes

This week's games offer questions about quarterback availability, defensive pressure, running-game identity, and how teams adapt when key players are limited.

Week 5 matchups

Early football questions, not final verdicts. Availability and team roles may change during the week.

Buccaneers at Cowboys

Dallas has a recognizable passing identity built around Dak Prescott and CeeDee Lamb. Tampa Bay's question is whether its supporting pieces can keep the afternoon competitive amid uncertainty at quarterback.

Eagles at Jaguars

Jacksonville's defensive progress meets Philadelphia's familiar offensive talent. Can the Jaguars impose pressure, or will the Eagles restore their rhythm?

Raiders at Patriots

Las Vegas has Brock Bowers, Ashton Jeanty and a disruptive defense. New England has to show that its structure can handle those strengths. This is one of the more intriguing matchups on the page.

Texans at Titans

Houston's offensive foundation meets a Tennessee team looking for ways to disrupt routine possessions. Which team establishes the tempo?

Browns at Jets

Breece Hall's availability and workload shape the Jets' possibilities. Garrett Wilson offers explosiveness, while Cleveland has an opportunity to challenge the supporting structure.

Colts at Steelers

Jonathan Taylor's production meets a Pittsburgh defense capable of imposing difficult terms. Can Indianapolis find enough ways to move the ball?

Giants at Commanders

Cam Skattebo provides a focal point for New York. Washington's quarterback availability could substantially change the matchup's shape.

Bengals at Dolphins

Cincinnati's receiving talent is the main attraction, subject to availability. Miami will try to keep that passing rhythm from settling in.

Vikings at Saints

Minnesota's defense and special teams have supplied more confidence than its offense. Can New Orleans demand more from the Vikings' attacking unit?

Bears at Packers

Chicago's quarterback situation is a test of how much of the team's recent progress can transfer. Green Bay's receiving talent presents a different challenge.

Broncos at Chargers

Denver's defensive secondary and the Chargers' protection questions create a compelling contest of strengths and vulnerabilities.

Lions at Cardinals

Detroit's offense, especially Jahmyr Gibbs, provides a clear identity. Arizona needs to change the pace rather than merely trade scores.

49ers at Seahawks

Brock Purdy and San Francisco's offensive structure meet Jaxon Smith-Njigba and a Seattle defense that has earned attention. Which side can sustain its preferred style?

Ravens at Falcons

Lamar Jackson's availability could change the entire story. Bijan Robinson gives Atlanta an established offensive center regardless.

Bills at Rams

Josh Allen gives Buffalo an explosive dimension; the Rams bring a variety of offensive pieces. Can Los Angeles make this a contest of sustained possessions?
